# Google Business Profile — ready to paste

Everything below is written to be copied straight into the form at
**https://business.google.com**, signed in as `hopehomeoclinic15@gmail.com`.

Why this matters more than anything else left on the list: the clinic's map
pin is currently a bare coordinate, not a registered business. Someone in
Purnea searching *"homeopathy doctor near me"* or *"होम्योपैथी डॉक्टर पूर्णिया"*
will not find Hope Homeo Clinic today. The website cannot fix that from the
outside — only this listing can. It is free.

## Description

Google's limit is 750 characters. This one is within it — paste as is.

```
Hope Homeo Clinic is a homoeopathic clinic at Jhanda Chowk on Khazanchi Road, Purnia, run by Dr. Chandani Kumari, B.H.M.S. (B.U.), Reg. No. 36342.

Dr. Chandani Kumari sees every patient herself and takes a full case history — the complaint, sleep, appetite, past illness — before deciding on a remedy. Medicines are dispensed at the clinic, so there is nothing separate to buy outside. Patients are seen by appointment or as walk-ins, in order of arrival.

Concerns commonly seen here include hair fall, P.C.O.D., menstrual problems, piles, kidney stone, joint pain, skin conditions such as eczema and psoriasis, and child health.

Open Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 5 pm. Sunday closed.
```

Do not add claims about curing conditions. Google's healthcare guidelines
and India's Drugs & Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act 1954
both restrict that, and the description above is deliberately written to
describe what happens at a consultation rather than promise an outcome.

## Verification

Google will offer one of: a **postcard** posted to the clinic with a code
(usually 5–14 days in Bihar), a **phone call**, or a **video call** where you
walk through the clinic showing the signboard, the consulting room and
something proving you run it.

**Take the video option if offered** — it verifies in days rather than weeks.
Have ready: the clinic signboard, the street outside, the consulting room,
the medicine cabinet, and Dr. Chandani Kumari's B.H.M.S. registration
certificate (Reg. No. 36342).

The listing is not live until verification completes. Nothing you enter
appears in Google Maps before then.

## After it goes live

**1. Photos — this is what decides whether people call.** Listings with
photos get substantially more calls than listings without. Add:

- The clinic signboard, readable, straight on
- The building entrance from the street, so patients recognise it
- The consulting room
- The waiting area
- The dispensary / medicine shelves
- Dr. Chandani Kumari at work (with her agreement)
- Your logo as the profile picture — `assets/logo-gold.png` is in this repo

**2. Reviews.** Ask patients who are happy, in person, at the end of a
follow-up. Google will give you a short review link — save it in your phone
and send it on WhatsApp while they are still at the clinic. Never offer
anything in exchange for a review; Google removes those and can penalise
the listing.

**3. Tell me when it is verified.** Once the listing exists, the map on the
website can point at the real Google place instead of a bare coordinate, so
the pin will show "Hope Homeo Clinic" inside Google's own map rather than
only on the card the site draws over it.
