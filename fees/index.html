Questions

Asked at the clinic every week

How long is a consultation?

A first consultation usually takes twenty to thirty minutes, because the history is taken in detail. Follow-ups are shorter.

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk-in patients are seen in the order they arrive. An appointment only saves you waiting time.

Are medicines given at the clinic?

Yes. Medicines are dispensed here, so there is no need to go to a chemist afterwards. They are charged separately from the ₹200 consultation fee.

Is there a fee for a follow-up visit?

No. If you come back within 45 days of your last visit, the follow-up is free. After 45 days it is charged as a fresh consultation at ₹200. Medicines are charged separately either way.

How many follow-ups will I need?

It depends on the complaint. Most patients are reviewed once a month, and the plan is discussed with you at the first visit.

Can homoeopathy be taken with my other medicines?

Usually yes, but tell Dr. Chandani Kumari everything you are taking. Never stop a prescribed allopathic medicine on your own.

Is it safe for children and during pregnancy?

Doses for children are small and pleasant to take. If you are pregnant, say so at the start of the consultation.

Will the complaint come back after stopping?

Some complaints settle for good, others need review during seasonal change. This is discussed honestly at follow-up rather than promised in advance.

What if my complaint needs a specialist?

You will be told plainly at the first visit and referred. Emergencies and cases needing hospital care are not treated here.
