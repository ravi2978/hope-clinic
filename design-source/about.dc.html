About Hope Homeo Clinic

Hope Homeo Clinic is located at Jhanda Chowk, on Khazanchi Road in Purnea. Dr. Chandani Kumari sees every patient herself, six days a week, and dispenses medicines at the clinic.

Dr. Chandani Kumari

Homoeopathic Physician · B.H.M.S. (B.U.) · Reg. No. 36342

Dr. Kumari qualified in homoeopathic medicine and has practised in Purnea since. The clinic keeps to one doctor and one consulting room, so there is enough time in every appointment to take a proper history.

Medicines are dispensed at the clinic, so there is nothing separate to buy outside. If a complaint needs a specialist or hospital care, you are told at the first visit and referred, rather than kept on treatment for months.
