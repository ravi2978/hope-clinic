Hair fall

01
बालों का झड़ना

Thinning or shedding that has continued for weeks or months, often with a change in scalp condition. Causes range from anaemia and thyroid problems to childbirth, illness or long stress.

Symptoms patients come with
—Hair coming out while combing or washing
—Widening parting or thinning at the crown
—Itching, flaking or an oily scalp
—Brittle hair that breaks before it grows
What to bring
✓Any blood report from the last six months
✓A list of medicines and supplements you take
✓A photograph of the parting, if the change is recent
At the consultation

Dr. Chandani Kumari looks at the scalp, asks how long the shedding has gone on, and takes a history of diet, periods, sleep, illness and recent medicines. Blood reports are reviewed if you have them.

Follow-up

A review in four weeks, then usually monthly. Hair responds slowly, so change is judged over months rather than weeks.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Protein at both meals, green leafy vegetables, one vitamin-C fruit daily. Avoid skipping meals and crash diets.

P.C.O.D.

02
पी.सी.ओ.डी.

Polycystic ovarian disease, where the ovaries do not release an egg regularly. It commonly shows up as irregular periods together with weight, skin or hair changes.

Symptoms patients come with
—Periods that come late, or are skipped
—Weight gain that is hard to shift
—Acne, or hair on the face and chin
—Difficulty conceiving
What to bring
✓Ultrasound or hormone reports, if done
✓Dates of your last three periods
✓Any medicine already prescribed for P.C.O.D.
At the consultation

The consultation covers cycle dates, weight history, family history and any scans or hormone reports. Remedy selection takes the whole picture into account, not the scan alone.

Follow-up

Monthly, timed around your cycle, so the pattern can be followed over three to six cycles.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Whole grains instead of maida, a protein at every meal, thirty minutes of walking. Avoid sugar and sweetened drinks.

Menstrual problems

03
महावारी समस्या

Pain, heavy or scanty bleeding, long gaps, or bleeding between periods. Many of these settle once the underlying pattern is understood.

Symptoms patients come with
—Cramping that stops normal work
—Very heavy or very light flow
—Clots, or bleeding for more than seven days
—Mood or sleep changes before periods
What to bring
✓Dates of your last three periods
✓Any pelvic scan report
✓A note of the pain — where it starts and what eases it
At the consultation

You will be asked to recall the last three cycle dates, the flow, and the pain — where it sits and what eases it. Please bring any scan reports.

Follow-up

One review per cycle for the first three cycles.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Iron-rich food through the week, warm meals during the period, adequate water. Limit tea with meals.

Piles

04
पाइल्स

Swollen veins in and around the anus, usually from long-standing constipation or straining. Most cases settle with treatment and a steady change in diet.

Symptoms patients come with
—Bleeding while passing stool
—Pain, itching or a lump at the opening
—A feeling of incomplete emptying
—Discomfort on sitting for long
What to bring
✓A note of your usual bowel habit
✓Any ointment or medicine used so far
✓Any earlier opinion you have been given
At the consultation

Bowel habit, water intake, diet and how long the complaint has lasted are all discussed. An examination is done only with your consent, and dietary advice is given alongside the remedy.

Follow-up

Two weeks, then monthly. A diet chart is given at the first visit.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Soaked figs or raisins in the morning, high-fibre meals, ten to twelve glasses of water. Avoid chillies, pickles and long straining.

Kidney stone

05
किडनी स्टोन

Hard deposits that form in the kidney and may move into the ureter. Size and position, seen on ultrasound, decide what is possible.

Symptoms patients come with
—Sharp pain in the back or side that comes in waves
—Burning or frequent urination
—Blood in the urine
—Nausea with the pain
What to bring
✓Ultrasound or CT report showing stone size
✓Urine routine report, if done
✓A note of when the pain comes and how long it lasts
At the consultation

Ultrasound reports are reviewed first. A large stone, or one that is blocking the flow, is referred to a specialist — that is said plainly at the first visit.

Follow-up

Fortnightly during an episode, with a repeat scan as advised.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Three litres of water spread through the day, lemon water, normal dietary calcium. Avoid very salty food and cola.

Joint pain

06
जोड़ों का दर्द

Pain, stiffness or swelling in one or many joints, from wear, injury, or an inflammatory condition.

Symptoms patients come with
—Morning stiffness that eases with movement
—Swelling or warmth over a joint
—Pain climbing stairs or squatting
—Pain that shifts from joint to joint
What to bring
✓X-ray or MRI report, if done
✓Blood reports such as RA factor or uric acid
✓A list of painkillers you use and how often
At the consultation

Which joints, at what time of day, and what makes the pain better or worse — cold, heat, rest, movement. These details guide the remedy closely.

Follow-up

Three to four weeks, longer once the pain settles.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Warm cooked food, turmeric with milk, gentle daily movement. Limit very cold and heavily fried food.

Skin conditions

07
चर्म रोग

Long-standing eczema, psoriasis, urticaria, fungal patches and acne. Skin complaints usually need patience and a steady course.

Symptoms patients come with
—Itching that disturbs sleep
—Dry, scaling or cracked patches
—Rash that comes and goes with seasons
—Pigmentation left after healing
What to bring
✓Photographs of the skin during a flare
✓Names of creams and tablets used before
✓Any allergy or patch-test report
At the consultation

The consultation covers when the skin first changed, what worsens it, earlier ointments used, and family history. Photographs of a flare are useful.

Follow-up

Monthly. Skin often looks worse before it improves, and this is explained at the start.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Plenty of water, seasonal fruit, plain home food while the skin settles. Note and avoid foods that trigger a flare.

Child health

08
शिशु रोग

Repeated colds and cough, poor appetite, weak digestion, bedwetting, and behaviour or sleep complaints in children.

Symptoms patients come with
—Falling ill every few weeks
—Refusing food, or very slow weight gain
—Frequent cold, cough or ear pain
—Bedwetting past the usual age
What to bring
✓Immunisation card
✓Growth chart or weight record
✓A note of how often the child falls ill
At the consultation

Parents do most of the talking. Birth history, milestones, appetite, sleep and school life are covered. Doses for children are small and pleasant to take.

Follow-up

Monthly, or sooner during an illness.

Diet advice
Full diet chart →

Fixed meal times, milk and dal daily, fruit instead of packaged snacks. Avoid feeding while distracted by a screen.
