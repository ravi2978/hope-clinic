Remedies and research

The remedies, in plain language

Ten well-known remedies and what they are traditionally used for, with published literature listed underneath and linked to its source.

Homoeopathy is a system of traditional medicine and views on its effectiveness differ. Nothing on this page is a prescription — remedy and dose are decided in consultation.
Arnica montana
Arn.
Leopard’s bane

Traditionally used after falls, bruising and physical strain.

Commonly given for
Soreness after injury, aching muscles, before and after dental work when advised.
Nux vomica
Nux-v.
Poison nut

Traditionally used for indigestion, acidity and irritability after overwork.

Commonly given for
Acidity, constipation with urging, complaints after rich food or long hours.
Belladonna
Bell.
Deadly nightshade

Traditionally used for sudden fever with flushed skin and throbbing pain.

Commonly given for
Fever of sudden onset, throbbing headache, sore throat with redness.
Pulsatilla
Puls.
Wind flower

Traditionally used for changeable complaints, menstrual and childhood.

Commonly given for
Irregular or scanty periods, childhood colds, complaints better in open air.
Sulphur
Sulph.
Brimstone

Traditionally used for long-standing itching and dry skin complaints.

Commonly given for
Chronic itching worse for warmth, dry scaling skin, long-standing eruptions.
Rhus toxicodendron
Rhus-t.
Poison ivy

Traditionally used for stiffness that eases with continued movement.

Commonly given for
Joint stiffness worse on first moving, backache, pain after strain.
Bryonia alba
Bry.
White bryony

Traditionally used for pain worse from the slightest motion, with dryness.

Commonly given for
Joint pain that forbids movement, dry cough, headache worse on stooping.
Calcarea carbonica
Calc.
Calcium carbonate

Traditionally used for slow recovery, easy sweating and bone complaints.

Commonly given for
Slow-growing children, easy sweating of the head, weak bones and teeth.
Thuja occidentalis
Thuj.
White cedar

Traditionally used for warty skin growths and conditions of long standing.

Commonly given for
Warts and skin growths, oily skin, complaints of long standing.
Natrum muriaticum
Nat-m.
Common salt

Traditionally used for headache, dryness and long-held grief.

Commonly given for
Headache from sun or strain, dry lips and skin, complaints after loss.
