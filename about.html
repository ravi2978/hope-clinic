In plain language

What homoeopathy is, and what it is not

Homoeopathy works from the whole picture of a patient rather than the complaint alone. Two people with the same diagnosis may be given different remedies, because the way an illness shows itself differs from person to person.

Remedies are prepared from plant, mineral and animal sources in very small, repeatedly diluted doses. They are taken as small pills or drops, usually for a defined period, and reviewed at each follow-up.

Views on how effective homoeopathy is differ, and this clinic makes no claim to cure named diseases. What is offered is a careful consultation, a remedy chosen for your case, and an honest referral when a complaint needs a specialist or hospital care.
